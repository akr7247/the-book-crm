# Three-minute walkthrough — shot script

Record in a private window at 1440×900, browser zoom 100%. The page is public; no login
is needed. Keep `.env`, terminal windows and the repository's environment settings off
screen for the whole recording.

**Before recording:** open the hosted URL, click account A, then scroll to the top.
Speak at a normal pace; the timings below are a guide, not a race.

---

### 0:00 – 0:20 · What this is

**On screen:** the "Both accounts" view, hero and four stats.

> This is The Book: one page for my two accounts. Eastern Counties Police Alliance at
> three hundred and fifty thousand pounds a year, and Aldermoor Industrial Group at a
> hundred and forty thousand euros. Every number on this page links to the record it came
> from. If a record does not state it, the page says unknown.

### 0:20 – 0:55 · What is the book worth?

**On screen:** click A, point at the deal, then the declined block, then the right panel.

> Account A: one backed deal, three hundred and fifty thousand a year, ending
> twenty-eighth of February, with no automatic renewal. The three-hundred-dock tender and
> the thirty-dock stadium are on the page but carry no value, because no record prices
> them.
>
> The hundred and five thousand of offers the customer already declined sit separately.
> They are not pipeline. The same rule on B: one hundred and forty thousand backed, two
> hundred and eighty thousand declined and excluded.

### 0:55 – 1:25 · Who holds the power?

**On screen:** Q2, hover the seats, point at one empty seat.

> Each deal has four seats: who decides, who pays, who uses it, who can block it.
>
> At Eastern Counties the signatory is not named. Rhys Calder walks the renewal through,
> but he is not the signatory and he leaves in January. At Aldermoor, group finance owns
> the renewal and has never been contacted directly.
>
> The empty seats are the point. They are the work.

### 1:25 – 1:55 · What do you do first?

**On screen:** Q3, point at each owner and date.

> Three moves, each with one owner and a date inside the deadline it protects.
>
> First, a written answer to Inspector Karen Mbeki before her fifth-of-December public
> event. She asked on the eighteenth of November and nobody replied.
>
> Second, get Rhys Calder to name his successor and the procurement lead before the
> January handover. Third, send the renewal order and start the force-by-force review
> before the term ends.
>
> On B: call Beatriz Alonso about the three silent docks, send group finance the
> per-plant quote they asked for in October, and start the case study for the sister
> company.

### 1:55 – 2:25 · What is about to go wrong?

**On screen:** Q4, both accounts, point at the high risks.

> At Eastern Counties, seventeen of thirty-five docks flew nothing in the last four weeks.
> That is a hundred and seventy thousand pounds of annual value apportioned to silent
> kit, and it is the argument a force budget holder will make at renewal.
>
> At Aldermoor, three of the four busiest docks stopped flying in the week of the
> sixteenth of November. All three stayed online. Nobody at the customer has said
> anything. Thirty thousand euros of annual value is attached to them.

### 2:25 – 2:50 · What do you still not know?

**On screen:** Q5, scroll the gap list, point at the "who can fill it" column.

> Eight gaps per account, each with the record or the person who can fill it. Who signs
> the Eastern Counties renewal. Which dock sits at which force. Who is Rhys's successor.
> Why seventeen docks are idle.
>
> At Aldermoor: why the three docks stopped, who Stefan Roy's deputy is, and which dock
> sits at which plant. The page does not guess any of these.

### 2:50 – 3:10 · Why you can trust it

**On screen:** click a citation in Q1, show the source drawer. Close it. Click "All
sources". Point at the live strip.

> Click any figure and the record opens beside it: the contract, the email, the usage row,
> the ticket. All one hundred and forty records are listed in one place.
>
> The signals strip is read through the MCP once a minute on the full server, and the
> hosted copy refreshes on a schedule. Each signal is delivered once, so the page keeps
> them as dated events. Notes go back through write_note.

### 3:10 – 3:20 · Close

**On screen:** back to the top, both accounts.

> That is the book I would hand my manager on Monday: what is real, what is claimed, what
> is missing, and what I do first. Thank you.
